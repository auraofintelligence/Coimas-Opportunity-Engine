04 · Evidence and boundaries

How the engine thinks

Separate the facts from the decision. World Travel Patterns maps public jurisdiction information. Project Atlas maps the public repository collection. Coimas curates distinct initiatives and their proposed engagement requirements. A country record marked “mapped” does not establish suitability.

Unknown is unknown. Eligibility gates must be reviewed as met, at least 60% of weighted requirements must be reviewed, and conflicting evidence prevents ranking. Research-source coverage measures available starting material, not whether a requirement is met. Fees are selectable sourced items, not an automatic all-in budget.

Respect local governance. Local assets stay locally anchored. International manufacturing, research or replication need separate scopes and permissions. C-Hours record verified voluntary human contribution only, without monetary value.
